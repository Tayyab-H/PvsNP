# C-304 - Exact algebraic semantics for native support proofs; scalar span bridge fails

Date: 28 September 2026  
Classification: **EXACT REPRESENTATION / ALGEBRAIC ROUTE FILTER.** This identifies a faithful finite-dimensional algebraic representation of the C-281 support grammar and proves that no nonzero scalar valuation into a field can preserve its alternative/composition operations. It gives no new bound on q.

## 1. Support semiring

Let `Lambda=[N] x {0,1}` be the signed literals. A support is a subset of `Lambda` containing at most one literal per coordinate. Write `S <=_ext T` when `T` is a consistent extension of `S`. A proof family is represented by its upward closure `U` among consistent supports; equivalently, by its minimal antichain `Min(U)`.

Define

```text
U + V = upward-closure(U union V)
U * V = upward-closure({S union T : S in U, T in V, S union T consistent}).
```

The zero is the empty family and the one is the family of all consistent supports (the upward closure generated by the empty support). This is the C-281 compatible-support semiring in upward-closed form: `+` is alternative proof, `*` joins two compatible proofs. Each C-281 rule is one product of two sums; the least fixed point gives its cyclic semantics.

## 2. Faithful monomial-ideal realization

Over any field `K`, form the `K`-algebra `A_N` with basis `m_S` for every consistent support `S` and multiplication

```text
m_S m_T = m_(S union T), if S union T is consistent;
          0,             otherwise.
```

This is associative: a product is nonzero exactly when the total union is consistent. There are exactly `3^N` basis monomials (each coordinate is absent, fixed to 0, or fixed to 1).

For an upward-closed support family `U`, let

```text
I(U) = span_K {m_S : S in U}.
```

Then `I(U)` is an ideal, and the map `U -> I(U)` is faithful. It preserves both operations:

```text
I(U + V) = I(U) + I(V)
I(U * V) = I(U) I(V).
```

For the product identity, every nonzero product of basis monomials records a compatible union. Conversely, if `R` extends a compatible union `S union T`, upward closure puts `R` in the generated ideal, and `m_R=m_R m_T` with `m_R in I(U)` and `m_T in I(V)`. Thus the full native antichain grammar has an exact representation by ideal-valued equations in an algebra of dimension `3^N`.

If `ell(w)={(i,w_i): i in [N]}` is a complete table support and `U_out` is the output family, then

```text
the native separator accepts w  iff  ell(w) is in U_out
                                  iff  m_ell(w) is in I(U_out).
```

This is a precise linear-algebraic semantics for C-281, but it is **not** a classical monotone span program: the tested target monomial `m_ell(w)` depends on all N input bits, whereas a span program has a fixed target and input-selected rows. The ambient algebra also has exponential dimension.

## 3. Why a scalar field valuation cannot be the missing bridge

The support semiring is idempotent: `x+x=x` for every element. Suppose `phi` is a unital semiring homomorphism from it to a field. Then

```text
phi(x) + phi(x) = phi(x+x) = phi(x).
```

Since field addition is cancellative, `phi(x)=0` for every `x`, including `x=1`, contradicting `phi(1)=1`. More generally, every additive map from this idempotent semiring into a cancellative additive group is zero. Therefore a proposed GF(2) owner-mask or field-valued potential that is claimed to preserve both OR-alternative and proof-composition as scalar addition/multiplication cannot exist.

This is narrower than ruling out span programs. A surviving bridge could use subspaces/ideals, tensors, or a non-homomorphic encoding. It must explicitly handle the input-dependent full-table monomial and avoid paying the `3^N` ambient dimension.

## 4. Rank and dimension do not yet help

Taking the dimension of `I(U)` is not a q-sensitive invariant: ideal addition satisfies `dim(I+J)` with overlap-dependent behavior, while ideal multiplication can change dimension sharply, and no bound relates either to the number of native states. Even the top-degree component fails generically. In the C-257 even-versus-odd calibration, the output accepts all `2^(N-1)` even tables; the corresponding full-support monomials are linearly independent, yet the native cover has `4N-4` pairs. Thus high rank of the accepted full-table slice coexists with linear q.

The representation does preserve the information erased by raw `GF(2)` rank: each basis monomial records a whole compatible partial assignment, and ideal multiplication records a compatible splice. A potentially useful invariant would have to measure how low-circuit descriptions select these state-indexed ideals or how their product tensor factors, not merely the dimension of their span. It must pass both C-257 parity and C-258 equality.

## 5. Disposition

The direct scalar-field valuation route is closed. C-281's compatible-support semantics can be embedded faithfully into a `3^N`-dimensional monomial algebra, but the construction does not compress the algebra, yield a conventional span program, or charge q. Keep O-167 only for a concrete compressed ideal/tensor invariant coupled to actual low-circuit descriptions; otherwise focus on O-168's full-promise `N^(1+o(1))` cover and global description coherence. The proved native lower bound remains `q=N-o(N)`.
