The exact shrink test is expensive: for fixed Q, whether C stays 0 on the entire Q-subcube is coNP; finding Q of size at most t is Sigma2^P. Also C may accept middle-band points, whereas a generic OPS anti-checker only excludes Low circuits. Either prove an OPS-short certificate for every High input of every valid separator, give an explicit valid separator/extension showing the claim false, or replace certificate size by a different separator-sensitive quantity with a total-gate inequality. Test parity, repeated-block equality, sparse parity checks, and global relations. No current frontier change.

**C-422 refinement.** A short semantic anti-checker exists for every High input: Lipton-Young minimax and majority give an address multiset of size O(N^beta) at the OPS parameters. This does not shorten the separator's zero-certificate, because middle-band completions may still be accepted. The remaining problem is separator-sensitive computation of a short sample, not its abstract existence.

## O-242 - Can a separator compute a fractional anti-checker strategy?

C-422 proves that for every High table f there is a distribution mu on addresses with mismatch probability at least 1/4 against every size-s1 circuit, and a multiset of O(N^beta) addresses sampled from mu anti-checks all Low tables. The proof uses majority composition and minimax; it does not provide a map f -> mu or f -> Q. Investigate whether an arbitrary total separator DAG computes such a strategy or sample with O(S+N^(1+O(beta))) total gates, without assuming a description output, address-by-address testing, or exclusion of accepted middle-band tables. The direct best response is an NP search over low circuit descriptions; enumerating them costs 2^(O(N^beta)). Any proposed transfer must charge selector gates, list output bits, shared truth-table lookup, verifier gates, wires, and description bits separately. Pair the attempt with a full-promise separator; do not count the classical existence theorem as an ordinary circuit lower bound. OPS and native rho frontiers stay separate.
**O-242 direct LP attempt.** The exact N-by-M payoff matrix has M=2^(O(N^beta)) Low functions; materializing it costs O(NM) gates, and a generic exact LP circuit is polynomial in NM, still 2^(O(N^beta)). This computes a selector only at exponential-in-N^beta size, not the OPS target. Retire direct matrix enumeration as the computational bridge; the live target is to use the separator's actual gate DAG or a genuinely constructive approximation method without enumerating Low circuits.

**C-423 boundary-pivot attempt.** The first accepting coordinate along a random-order path from a rejected input to a uniform Low endpoint is not a generic anti-checker: a two-branch sparse family makes almost all pivots lie in A while an exceptional Low table disagrees only on disjoint B, with hit probability 2^-t. This does not instantiate an OPS-High input because the toy's f=0 is Low. Do not repair by sharper concentration; any surviving version needs a proved actual-High-specific coverage property.

## O-243 - Can actual High circuit complexity force boundary-pivot coverage?

Given a valid Gap-MCSP separator C and a High table f, is there an endpoint distribution over Low tables, computable from C with a charged ordinary-gate cost, such that the first-acceptance pivot law anti-checks every Low table at constant probability? C-423 refutes the uniform endpoint distribution from generic distance/entropy facts, but not this High-specific statement. A proof must use CC(f)>=s2 beyond minimum Hamming distance, account for path evaluation and endpoint selection, and handle every Low table without assuming a witness circuit output. Treat this as a one-shot transfer test, not a target for repeated lemma refinements. If it fails, retire pivots and switch to a direct total-gate mechanism on the separator DAG; do not restate the generic pivot lemma. The paired linear-sketch candidate has rank at least N-O(N^beta n) and leaves nonlinear Low membership unresolved. Ordinary and native frontiers remain unchanged.
**C-424 refinement to O-243.** The pivot test is retired as a generic gate mechanism. C-424 has a genuinely High f and an O(N)-gate filter that rejects every High table while accepting an exponential selected Low family; its pivot law still misses a special Low table with probability o(1). The filter fails Low-side completeness. Any full-separator pivot theorem must use a consequence of accepting *all* Low tables, not just circuit hardness, endpoint entropy, distance, or global soundness. Do not keep refining local path distributions.

## O-245 - Charge the ordinary complete sound certificate-cover DAG

For any full Gap-MCSP separator C, its 1-certificates are subcubes contained in `SIZE(<s2)`, each with at most `O(N^beta n)` free coordinates, and their union covers `SIZE(s1)`. Prove a total-gate lower bound for a shared AND/OR/NOT DAG generating such a cover, or construct a near-linear DAG that covers the entire Low class while remaining inside `SIZE(<s2)`. A proof must charge reuse among certificate cubes; counting cubes, widths, or Low descriptions alone yields at most sublinear `Omega(N^beta/polylog N)` information. Test parity, repeated-block equality, sparse parity checks, and global relations. Preserve the middle band and keep wires, description bits, and construction time separate. This is the ordinary total-gate target, not the native q obligation O-244. Current lower bounds and exact enumeration remain unchanged.

**O-245 status after C-425: certificate-multiplicity route retired.** Hamming-weight threshold has an `O(N)` shared population-count DAG while requiring `binom(N,u)` safe cubes for its weight-u layer. The complete-cover problem remains open only if recast around a different gate-semantic mechanism.

## O-246 - Find an operation-wise gate potential for complete Low recognition

For ordinary fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT DAGs with unrestricted fan-out, define and prove a potential whose change under each gate operation (including reusing an existing gate output) is bounded by a constant, but whose value on every circuit separating `SIZE(s1)` from `CC>N^beta` is `Omega(N^(1+epsilon))` for some fixed epsilon and all sufficiently small fixed beta. The potential cannot be only: number or width of certificates, essential inputs, queried coordinates, Hamming-ball volume, total influence, or a sum charging each occurrence independently. The C-425 population counter defeats certificate multiplicity; C-424 parity, repeated-block equality, sparse parity checks, and simple global block relations defeat incidence-only charges. Pair the proof attempt with a complete near-linear separator attempt. If no operation-wise recurrence and output lower bound can be proved, retire the candidate instead of strengthening it rhetorically. Preserve separate accounting for gates, wires, descriptions, construction time, and native fusion states. Current quantitative frontier is unchanged.

**C-426 closeout:** `E(C)`, the essential-input potential, is exhausted. Every separator has `E(C)>=N-O(N^beta*n)`, and the explicit projected-Low separator has `E(C)<=N-c*N^beta*n` for a constant c>0. Thus the minimum possible essential support is `N-Theta(N^beta*n)`; support cannot supply a superlinear value. Do not repackage support/cylinder width as a new gate potential.

## O-248 - Closed: rnKt threshold is not an OPS promise separator

C-428 proves that for each fixed 0<beta<1, OPS-YES contains tables with rnKt Omega(N^beta/log N), while infinitely many OPS-NO tables have rnKt=2^((log N)^gamma)=N^o(1) for any fixed 0<gamma<1. Neither monotone rnKt cutoff orientation classifies the promise: decreasing cutoffs fail on high-rnKt YES and low-rnKt NO; increasing cutoffs fail on zero YES and a counting-chosen high-rnKt NO. C-419, C-420, and C-426 already refute the simplest balanced-block, linear-code, and safe-cylinder embeddings. ## O-249 - Closed as a standalone route: gate-transcript 2-CNF fibers

## O-250 - Closed: cut communication rank has only a linear output ceiling

For a fixed input cut, every `S`-gate fan-in-two DAG gives a one-way communication protocol of at most `2S+1` bits, hence `rank_GF(2)(M_F)<=2^(2S+1)`. This fully allows sharing, but `log rank<=N/2` by matrix dimension. The cap is attained by an `N-1`-gate circuit `AND_i(x_i OR y_i)`. Uniform averaging over cuts preserves the same linear ratio. Parity, repeated-block equality, O(N)-incidence sparse parity checks, and equal-block-parity constraints also have O(N) shared circuits. Retire rank, rank averages, and unnormalized rank sums over cuts as standalone superlinear mechanisms; they do not refute an OPS separator lower bound. The paired full-promise Low-circuit enumerator remains O(N*2^(O(N^beta))) gates, and the trie attempt proves no compression. Continue with a promise-specific semantic property beyond generic cut rank. No frontier change. Proof and resource ledger: research/C435_COMMUNICATION_RANK_CHARGES_SHARING_BUT_CANNOT_EXCEED_LINEAR_2026-10-01.md.
