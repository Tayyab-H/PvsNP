# C-245 — Marked antichain grammar for all accepting contexts

Date: 27 September 2026  
Route: O-153, exact generation of the C-244 state zones.  
Status: exact context grammar and 2q-round zone-equivalent context bound; no quantitative q-charge.

## 1. Why mark a hole

C-244 reduces global sharing to the paired languages P_i (proof supports rooted at i) and K_i (outside supports of accepting contexts with a hole at i). Counting those families independently loses the fact that they come from one rule grammar. This note gives an exact grammar that generates proofs and one-hole contexts together.

## 2. The antichain support algebra

Work with antichains of consistent signed-literal supports. For families A,B define

    A plus B = Min(A union B),
    A times B = Min{a union b : a in A, b in B, a union b consistent}.

The zero is the empty family and the one is {empty support}. Addition means an alternative proof; multiplication means both side obligations must be supplied. Minimization preserves exactly which legal inputs match at least one support.

For state j and side S in {E,H}, let

    R_j^S(t) = Seed_j^S plus sum_{k in D_j^S} P_k(t),
    P_j(0) = zero,
    P_j(t+1) = R_j^E(t) times R_j^H(t).

Here Seed_j^S is the antichain of singleton seed supports and D_j^S is the predecessor set. Thus P_j(t) is the minimal-support family for proofs of height at most t, matching C-242.

## 3. One-hole context recurrence

Fix a target state i. Let K_{j,i}(t) be the minimal supports outside one distinguished occurrence of i in a proof rooted at j, with context height at most t. Initialize

    K_{j,i}(0) = one, if j=i,
                 zero, otherwise.

A hole can occur in exactly one of the two obligations at the root. Define the hole-side alternatives

    H_j^{S,i}(t) = sum_{k in D_j^S} K_{k,i}(t).

Then the exact recurrence is

    K_{j,i}(t+1) =
        (one if j=i, else zero)
        plus (H_j^{E,i}(t) times R_j^H(t))
        plus (R_j^E(t) times H_j^{H,i}(t)).

Every support in the second term chooses a context-bearing predecessor on the E side and an ordinary proof on the H side. The third term reverses the sides. The hole is not a seed leaf; seed alternatives occur only in the ordinary-proof factor R.

For output set O, define the bounded context family

    K_i^* = sum_{o in O} K_{o,i}(2q).

The exact zone identity needed by C-244 is

    [P_i(q)] AND [K_i] = [P_i(q)] AND [K_i^*],

where K_i on the left denotes all finite contexts. The 2q bound is enough even if an arbitrary context has a long cyclic path. If x matches a context K and a proof C rooted at i, substitution gives an accepting proof containing i. Choose a context path from an output root to the marked i occurrence with no repeated state: whenever a state repeats on that path, replace the earlier occurrence's context subtree by the later one, preserving the marked hole and deleting the intervening segment. The path then has at most q state visits. At each off-path branch, replace its proof by a rank-minimal proof on x, of height at most q. The resulting matching context has height at most 2q. Conversely every context generated by K_i^* is a valid context. Inclusion-minimalization discards a support only when a smaller support matches every input it matched, so the bounded family has the same zone after intersecting with [P_i(q)].

This is the antichain analogue of automatic differentiation: for a product, a single marked hole propagates through either factor, while the other factor remains an ordinary proof. It is also the coefficient-of-one-hole part of a proof polynomial; allowing two marked holes would add the corresponding cross terms.

## 4. Exact joint generation and state zones

The recurrence computes P and the zone-relevant K_i^* from the same rule grammar and same seed alphabet. With P_i=P_i(q), substitution then yields

    Acc_Q(x) = OR_i ( [P_i(q)](x) AND [K_i^*](x) ).

Each term is a safe state zone for a sound separator. The context grammar is a concrete algebraic representation of the reuse side of that factorisation; it is not an independent arbitrary family of subsets.

## 5. Complexity audit

For a fixed target i, the marked system has q proof families and q context families, each updated for at most 2q rounds. Across all targets i, a direct tabulation has q^2 context-family labels and 2q rounds. This is an exact representation-size bound for the *family indices*, not for explicit antichain contents, which may be exponentially large.

No useful q lower bound follows solely from the q^2 marked labels:

- the labels are derived from the same q-rule list and do not imply q^2 distinct supports;
- one family may have exponentially many incomparable or dominated derivation supports before minimization;
- C-228 permits safe singleton zones with readout hardness unrelated to dangerous splicing;
- C-234's diagonal equality cover has safe off-anchor joins and O(N) size.

The candidate quantitative object is the joint support-incidence tensor

    J(i,K,C) = 1 iff K is generated by K_i, C by P_i, and K union C is consistent.

Every nonzero entry gives a full output cylinder inside SIZE(s2). A lower bound must charge how the single q-rule grammar generates this tensor while its diagonal table coverage includes all SIZE(s1). Neither the number of its labels nor the number of finite derivation trees gives that charge.

No hostile check is contradicted by the recurrence; it is a formalization tool, not a lower-bound claim.
