# C-244 — Exact state-zone factorisation for native closure readout

Date: 27 September 2026  
Route: O-153, global context/proof sharing in the native cyclic fusion closure.  
Status: exact global semantic decomposition; no q-charge, near-linear cover, or P-vs-NP result.

## 1. Definitions

Fix a successful q-state closure on legal table inputs x in {0,1}^N. Let ell(x) be its matching signed-literal set.

For each state i:

- P_i is the family of finite proof supports rooted at i.
- K_i is the family of context supports obtained by cutting one occurrence of i out of a finite accepting proof rooted at an output rule. A context retains every seed leaf outside the cut occurrence.

For a support family F, let

    [F](x) = 1 iff there is A in F with A subset ell(x).

Write p_i(x)=[P_i](x), k_i(x)=[K_i](x), and Z_i={x:p_i(x)=k_i(x)=1}.

Proof trees can be used even when the implementation shares a DAG: unrolling a finite ranked derivation yields a finite tree, and substituting at its marked occurrence is exactly the C-242 reuse operation.

## 2. State-zone theorem

**Theorem.** On every legal table input,

    Acc_Q(x) = OR_{i=1}^q (p_i(x) AND k_i(x)).

Consequently, for a sound Gap-MCSP closure with Y=SIZE(s1) and U={0,1}^N minus SIZE(s2),

    Y subseteq union_i Z_i,
    Z_i intersect U = empty for every i,
    and therefore union_i Z_i subseteq SIZE(s2).

**Proof.**

If x is accepted, choose a finite accepting proof and any occurrence i in it (the root is allowed). Cutting the subproof at that occurrence gives a context support K in K_i; the removed subtree has some support C in P_i. Since the original proof matches x, both K and C are subsets of ell(x), so k_i(x)=p_i(x)=1.

Conversely, if k_i(x)=p_i(x)=1, choose matching supports K in K_i and C in P_i. Both are subsets of ell(x), hence K union C is consistent and still matches x. Substituting the proof rooted at i into the selected accepting context gives a finite accepting proof for x.

The equality follows. Completeness gives Y subseteq Acc_Q. Soundness gives Acc_Q intersect U empty. Each Z_i is a subset of Acc_Q by the equality, proving the remaining claims.

This is stronger than a statement about one chosen collision: the entire cross-product of all contexts and all replacement proofs at state i is represented by one safe semantic zone Z_i.

## 3. Certificate-cylinder consequence

For every K in K_i and C in P_i, if K union C is consistent, every table extending K union C lies in Z_i and is accepted. Soundness therefore gives

    Cyl(K union C) subseteq SIZE(s2).

In interval notation this repeats the C-242 endpoint law:

    ell(K union C)=ell(K) OR ell(C),
    u(K union C)=u(K) AND u(C),

and both endpoints must be size-s2 tables. The C-243 carrier containment also gives U intersect Cyl(C) subseteq T_i.

The high blocker has a compact semantic form here. For every z in U and every state i,

    NOT (p_i(z) AND k_i(z)).

Thus each high table blocks at least one side of every state zone, while every low table lies in at least one zone. This is a global factorisation of the accepting language into q proof/context intersections.

## 4. What the factorisation changes

The previous state-local formulation asked for an inequality on carrier volume, proof-cylinder packing, and context blocking. C-244 identifies the exact object those estimates would have to control: a cover of all low anchors by q safe zones Z_i, where each zone is the intersection of the language of proof supports rooted at i and the language of contexts that can use i.

The possible global lower-bound target is now:

    every legal q-state grammar covering SIZE(s1) by these safe state zones
    must have q >= N g(N), for some g(N) tending to infinity.

The dual upper-bound target is to synthesize these q zones, and their proof/context languages, in a full-promise cover with q=N polylog N or N^(1+o(1)).

This reformulation exposes the needed missing theorem precisely: bound the low-circuit mass/description entropy of a state zone in terms of the grammar that generates both sides of that zone. A bound on |T_i|, certificate width, number of anchors, or number of proof trees alone is insufficient.

## 5. Failed aggregation checks

1. **Counting the q zones alone fails.** A zone is not one cube. It is an intersection of two unions of exponentially many proof/context cylinders, and a q-state cyclic grammar can generate many derivations. No bound on |Z_i intersect SIZE(s1)| in terms of q has been established.
2. **Replacing zones by arbitrary safe subsets loses the model.** The only remaining structure is that both sides are generated by the same native grammar. A lower bound for arbitrary q safe sets cannot follow from coverage alone; it would need additional properties of the actual low/high promise.
3. **State collision is still insufficient.** C-228 has large readout complexity without a forbidden hybrid. C-234's diagonal equality cover safely reuses a scan state. Both remain mandatory counterchecks for any theorem about Z_i.
4. **Carrier volume does not multiply.** C-243 proves each high proof cylinder lies in T_i, but predecessor carriers can be correlated. Thus |T_j intersect T_k| cannot be bounded from |T_j| and |T_k| alone, and the state-zone factorisation supplies no independence.
5. **Raw profile counts remain too large.** C-238/C-239 allow many witness skeletons/rank profiles at q>=N; the exact q-zone decomposition does not reduce that count without a new constraint on the grammar-generated context/proof languages.

## 6. Exact frontier and next attempt

The next mathematical step is to analyze the *joint generation complexity* of (K_i,P_i), rather than count zones or certificates:

- derive a grammar for all context supports by marking one hole in a finite proof;
- determine the exact size/shape of its product with P_i under the antichain semiring;
- seek a measure on the diagonal coverage {(x,i): x in Z_i} that charges how many low descriptions one pair of context/proof languages can cover while keeping every cross-join inside SIZE(s2);
- test the candidate measure on C-80, C-160, C-228, C-234, and the C-230 safe-cube ceiling;
- in parallel keep searching for a direct N polylog N or N^(1+o(1)) full-promise cover.

This is a proved change in the global object of study, not a superlinear result. No actual-promise q lower bound beyond the established linear-scale floor, no near-linear cover, and no P-vs-NP proof has been obtained.
